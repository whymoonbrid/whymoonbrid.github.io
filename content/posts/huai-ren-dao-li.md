<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>济河泛舟楫</span> <span>息驾依城隅</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>白日匿西海</span> <span>长河没桑榆</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>践遗躅</span> <span>旷野侵栋宇</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>经穷巷</span> <span>蓬蒿翳旧居</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>对疏筠</span> <span>石泉过空闾</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>逝水如驱</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>丝桐吟清响</span> <span>修竹和秋商</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>大块噫高风</span> <span>天籁散苍茫</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>归鸿杳</span> <span>五弦度幽篁</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>长吟发</span> <span>置酒盈羽觞</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>万物扰</span> <span>游心寄俯仰</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>浮萍横江</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>弦歌辍</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>玉山倾</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>朱实堕</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>曩游尽</span> <span>故俦索</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>形骸酩酊</span> <span>失路蹉跎</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>出东郭</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>飙尘忽</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>去日多</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>素琴冷</span> <span>玄鹤过</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>白马靡归</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>平林月落</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>陵谷忽横奔</span> <span>众星拱北宸</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>俟河清讵可</span> <span>黄犬叹东门</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>天难谌</span> <span>将罪乎斯文</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>三千冠</span> <span>颠踣叩高阍</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>顾日影</span> <span>琅杆伏质刎</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>绝弦摧轸</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>策马趋洛京</span> <span>束带诣承明</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>羁鸟婴世网</span> <span>池鱼步金庭</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>登西第</span> <span>八佾列宫楹</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>过麦黍</span> <span>周粟委殷鼎</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>察尧心</span> <span>鹪鹩伏涕零</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>樗散何幸</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>橐龠张</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>大钧转</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>柳帷长</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>金魄醒</span> <span>白虹藏</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>声度邙阪</span> <span>熛散天章</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>含光净</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>承影游</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>宵练扬</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>竹下风</span> <span>炉间光</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-short"><span>夜夜犹作</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>广陵商</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>何醉欤</span> <span>行行径赴崦嵫前</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>何济欤</span> <span>沧浪水尽心中煎</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>何托欤</span> <span>灼灼邓林照素弦</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>何归欤</span> <span>弭节长向不周天</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>班马犯北风</span> <span>倦鸟失南木</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>邻笛槁簧里</span> <span>援翰向隅处</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>去者为壤骨</span> <span>生者同风烛</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>妙响天地去</span> <span>迅逝宁相属</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>游鱼杳濠水</span> <span>秋潦下溟波</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>大鹏戢南翼</span> <span>斥䳛渺深柯</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>寸阴息</span> <span>遗声寒寥廓</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>四海濯</span> <span>无何有薜萝</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>朝暮乖</span> <span>魂乎曷所措</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>逍遥焉托</span></p>
</div>
<div class="poem-hrl-stanza">
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>停驾言将迈</span> <span>徙倚临通衢</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>白日匿西海</span> <span>长河没桑榆</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>延陵剑</span> <span>遥遥向空予</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>接舆歌</span> <span>来者安可趋</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line"><span>思旧笛</span> <span>何独山阳曲</span></p>
<p class="poem-hrl-line poem-hrl-cadence"><span>北徂路遥孰与俱</span></p>
</div>
